Observations

In this exercise, MPI_Scatter distributes equal chunks of the complete array to all processes, and each process calculates the sum of its assigned chunk. MPI_Scan then performs a prefix reduction, so each process receives the cumulative sum of all local sums from rank zero through its own rank. The prefix sum is different on every process, and subtracting the local sum from it produces sum_before_me, which represents the total contributed by all earlier ranks. Each process verifies its prefix using the formula K multiplied by K plus one, divided by two, where K is the final array value handled by that rank. The last process receives the global total of 500,000,500,000 as its prefix sum. Unlike MPI_Allreduce, which gives the same global result to every process, MPI_Scan is useful when processes require cumulative values or global offsets based on their rank order.
