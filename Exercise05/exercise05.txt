Observations

In this exercise, MPI_Scatter distributes an equal portion of the complete array to each process, and every process calculates the sum of its local chunk. MPI_Allreduce then combines all local sums using MPI_SUM and makes the final total available to every process instead of only the root. As a result, each process can print its local sum, the global total of 500,000,500,000, and the percentage that its chunk contributes to the total. This removes the restriction of MPI_Reduce used in Exercise 4, where only the root could access the result. MPI_Allreduce is useful when all processes need the combined value and avoids using a separate broadcast operation after a reduction.
